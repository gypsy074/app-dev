### My favorite movie to watch this coming All soul's day or the Day of the Dead is  **Coco**.
> It is a Disney Animation that have a story that catches family's hearts. This story shows the day of Mexican holiday that is similar to Filipino holiday, Araw ng mga patay, that the dead loved ones still lives in the land of the dead. When their family stills remembers and pass the stories of their dead loved ones, their deceased family enjoys their after death life in the Land of the dead where there's a magical animals called Alebrijes, a guide spirit and colorful place city.
>
>  So the anual tradition of the day of the dead happens in November 1, every mexican or filipino put offerings such as food,  flower and candle in altar that have pictures of their dead family. They used the offerings to  guide them back to their home or family when they were alive.
